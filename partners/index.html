Your perspective.

Have your own datasets, environmental observations or research? We can develop an adaptation around them, alongside selected fromearth layers or as a dedicated experience.

Together, we review what the data can support, agree on its sources and permissions, and design how visitors explore it. Your team contributes the knowledge; we help make it tangible.

Integration is scoped with each partner. Sources, scenarios, units and uncertainties remain visible. Climate projections describe possible futures, not exact predictions for a street or a building.

Who we are.

Meet fromanother

Artists and storytellers creating shared experiences.

fromanother is an artist-led creative studio founded by Vicki Dang and Robin Mahieux. We bring together film, 3D art, design and creative technology to create digital and immersive experiences for institutions, cultural spaces and brands.

Our work begins with a story and the people it is for. We collaborate with curatorial, scientific and education teams to turn complex ideas into experiences that invite curiosity and connection.
