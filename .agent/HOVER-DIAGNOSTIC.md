# Country hover diagnostic — 2026-10-01

Baseline: d221411. User requested implementation of the filter-aware country hover previously discussed, with a corresponding touch interaction. Publication on main follows the user's existing preference.

The canvas diagnostic and mouse click use the country boundary. Explicit city labels and search continue to open cities. Without a filter, the tooltip displays the existing experimental country index /100 and the selected year. With a filter, it displays the corresponding physical measurement or population percentage, its unit, year and comparison reference. Climate readings reuse the population-weighted aggregation of available listed cities; this is stated in the tooltip and does not claim whole-territory coverage. Flood tooltips distinguish aggregated modelled depth from the map's flood-prone area. Missing readings stay missing; available zero remains zero.

Population uses the exact annual UN series: change mode compares with 2026; estimate mode compares with 2025, matching the existing map. The national population total is included. Climate deltas compare the displayed weighted level with the weighted 2026 baseline. Filter exclusivity, scientific data, sources and shaders are unchanged.

Touch pins a diagnostic, updates it when the year/filter/mode changes, and allows a second tap or Enter/Space to open its country sheet. Native compatibility clicks are accepted only when their pointerdown began on the tooltip/city label: labels revealed underneath the first canvas touch cannot accidentally open a sheet. Tooltip placement clamps to the viewport. Content/layout is cached across pointer movement and invalidates on relevant state, source and viewport changes.
